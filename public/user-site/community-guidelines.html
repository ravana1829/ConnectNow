Community Guidelines

ConnectNow Community Guidelines
ConnectNow is designed for respectful conversations, random chat, random video calls and social connections. Please treat every person with respect.

1. Be Respectful

Treat other users politely and respectfully. Do not intentionally insult, threaten, intimidate or harass another person.

2. No Harassment

  • No bullying or repeated unwanted contact.
  • No threats or intimidation.
  • No hate-based harassment.
  • No abusive or degrading behaviour.

3. No Illegal Activities

Do not use ConnectNow to facilitate or promote illegal activities.

4. Sexual and Explicit Content

Do not use ConnectNow to share or display sexually explicit, abusive, exploitative or otherwise prohibited content.

5. Protect Personal Information

Avoid sharing sensitive personal information such as:

  • Passwords
  • Bank or payment information
  • Private identification numbers
  • Exact home address
  • Other sensitive personal information

6. Impersonation

Do not intentionally impersonate another person, organization or public identity in a misleading way.

7. Spam and Abuse

  • No spam messages.
  • No malicious links.
  • No automated abuse of the platform.
  • No attempts to disrupt or damage the service.

8. Use Report and Block

If another user behaves inappropriately, use ConnectNow's Report and Block features.

9. Platform Safety

ConnectNow may take moderation or account actions when activity violates these guidelines or applicable platform rules.

10. Help Keep ConnectNow Safe

Everyone using ConnectNow contributes to the safety of the community. Be respectful, protect your privacy, and report serious problems.
