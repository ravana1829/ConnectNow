Privacy Policy

ConnectNow Privacy Policy
Your privacy matters to us. This page explains the types of information ConnectNow may collect and how that information may be used to operate and protect the service.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use ConnectNow, the service may process information such as:

  • Account identifier provided by the authentication service.
  • Display name or profile information you choose to provide.
  • Login method information, such as Google or Guest.
  • Messages and interaction information needed to operate the service.
  • Reports and blocking information used for safety.
  • Technical information needed to keep the service working.

2. Google Login

When you sign in with Google, ConnectNow receives authentication information made available by Firebase Authentication and Google, such as your account identifier and basic account information required for login.

3. Guest Login

Guest accounts use Firebase anonymous authentication. Guest users do not need to provide a Google account to access the basic login flow.

4. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate users.
  • To provide chat and communication features.
  • To maintain account and session functionality.
  • To process reports and blocking actions.
  • To detect abuse and protect the community.
  • To improve reliability and security.

5. Random Chat and Video Calls

Random communication features may require access to your microphone and camera when you choose to use voice or video functionality.

Browser permissions are controlled by your device and browser. You can deny or revoke these permissions through your browser settings.

6. Messages and Calls

ConnectNow is designed to provide real-time communication. Data handling and retention may depend on the specific feature, safety requirements and technical systems used by the service.

7. Reports and Blocking

When you report or block another user, information related to that action may be processed to help maintain platform safety and investigate violations.

8. Third-Party Services

ConnectNow may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, real-time communication, analytics, storage or other technical functions.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the service. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Choices

  • You may choose not to use Google Login.
  • You may use Guest Login where available.
  • You can control browser camera and microphone permissions.
  • You can stop using the service at any time.

11. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ConnectNow's features, technology and legal requirements change.

12. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the official support contact provided by ConnectNow.
